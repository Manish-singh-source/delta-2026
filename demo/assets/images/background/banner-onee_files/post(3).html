EECMAI Summit Kolkata 2025 – A Grand Success for Deltacab!
Date: 21st March 2025

Deltacab was honored to be a part of the EECMAI Summit Kolkata 2025, the leading industry event dedicated to the Elevator & Escalator industry. The summit brought together experts, manufacturers, and innovators to discuss advancements in safety, technology, and sustainability in the vertical transportation sector.

...

Exciting News!
We are thrilled to announce that
Our CEO, Mr. Aakarsh Khandhar, has been appointed as the Joint Secretary of EECMAI.

Key Takeaways from the Event:
Innovations in Elevator & Escalator Technology – Focus on efficiency, smart systems, and safety standards.
Expert Panel Discussions – Insights from industry leaders on the future of vertical mobility.
Networking & Collaborations – Engaging with key stakeholders to drive industry growth.

As a trusted name in cables and wiring solutions for elevators and escalators, Deltacab remains committed to driving innovation and safety in the industry.

A big thank you to the organizers, speakers, and attendees for making this event a huge success!
